❌ 錯題詳解（14 題）

Q1The commercial future of the company looks very ＿＿. (a. promoted　b. tactic　c. promising)
你選 A · promoted正解 C · promising
promising＝有希望的、大有可為的。「公司的商業前景看起來很有希望」。promoted 是「被升職／被推廣」，語意不合。
Q2We get most of our work through ＿＿ recommendations. (a. all in all　b. projected　c. word-of-mouth)
你選 B · projected正解 C · word-of-mouth
word-of-mouth＝口耳相傳的。「我們大部分的生意來自口耳相傳的推薦」。projected 是「預計的」。
Q3The class is mainly ＿＿ of Italian and French students. (a. disclosed　b. comprised　c. informed)
你選 A · disclosed正解 B · comprised
be comprised of＝由⋯⋯組成（固定用法）。「這個班級主要由義大利和法國學生組成」。disclosed 是「揭露」。
Q4I made some remark about his wife's family, and then realized I'd made a serious ＿＿. (a. cross section　b. faux pas　c. expenditure)
你選 C · expenditure正解 B · faux pas
faux pas（源自法文）＝失言、失禮的舉動。「我對他太太的家人說了些話，然後發現自己犯了嚴重的失禮」。expenditure 是「支出」。
Q6The company underwent ＿＿ and 1,500 workers lost their jobs. (a. restructuring　b. statisticulation　c. refreshment)
你選 C · refreshment正解 A · restructuring
restructuring＝（組織）重組。「公司進行重組，1,500 名員工失去工作」。refreshment 是「茶點」。
Q8A ＿＿ is a wall or screen that separates one part of a room or vehicle from another. (a. partition　b. parlour　c. click-through)
你選 B · parlour正解 A · partition
partition＝隔板、隔間牆。「隔板是用來分隔房間或車輛某部分的牆或屏風」。parlour 是「（美容院等）店舖」。
Q10The government recognized the ＿＿ need to maintain a stable currency. (a. overriding　b. transactional　c. domineering)
你選 C · domineering正解 A · overriding
overriding＝首要的、最重要的。「政府體認到維持貨幣穩定的首要需求」。domineering 是「盛氣凌人的」（形容人），不能修飾 need。
Q13It is possible to lead happy and productive lives ＿＿ their loss. (a. despite　b. in spite　c. more or less)
你選 C · more or less正解 A · despite
despite＝儘管（介系詞，後面直接接名詞）。「儘管失去了他們，仍能過著快樂而充實的生活」。陷阱：in spite 必須加 of（in spite of）才成立，單獨的 in spite 是錯的。
Q17The three groups will ＿＿ all with each other to help the child. (a. liaise　b. encrypt　c. delicate)
你選 B · encrypt正解 A · liaise
liaise＝聯繫、聯絡合作。「這三個團體將彼此聯繫合作來幫助這個孩子」。encrypt 是「加密」；delicate 是形容詞不能當動詞。
Q20You have all made compelling arguments, but ＿＿ that we need a viable, cost-effective solution... (a. the bottom line is　b. at short notice　c. rumour has it)
你選 B · at short notice正解 A · the bottom line is
the bottom line is (that)＝重點是、結論是。「但重點是我們需要一個可行又划算的解決方案」。at short notice 是「臨時、短時間內」。
Q22We should leave it to the military men to organize the ＿＿. (a. boot　b. tactics　c. decor)
你選 C · decor正解 B · tactics
tactics＝戰術、策略。「我們應該把規劃戰術的工作交給軍人」。decor 是「室內裝潢」，與軍人語境不合。
Q25Our fashion editor gives you the ＿＿ on winter coats for this season. (a. lowdown　b. perks　c. partition)
你選 C · partition正解 A · lowdown
the low-down on＝關於某事的內幕、最新重要情報。「我們的時尚編輯給你本季冬季大衣的第一手情報」。perks 是「福利」。
Q26Financial ＿＿ on the company are preventing them from employing new staff. (a. faux pas　b. privacy policy　c. constraints)
你選 A · faux pas正解 C · constraints
constraints＝限制、約束（financial constraints＝財務限制，固定搭配）。「公司的財務限制使他們無法雇用新員工」。
Q28British Airways is facing a record fine of £183m for last year's ＿＿ of its security systems. (a. breach　b. constraint　c. appraisal)
你選 C · appraisal正解 A · breach
breach＝違反、（安全）漏洞（breach of security＝資安漏洞）。「英國航空因去年的安全系統漏洞而面臨 1.83 億英鎊的破紀錄罰款」。appraisal 是「評估」。
